What's new in Lector Universal

Version 1.0.12

  • One general voice for the whole app (Settings, General, Read aloud) and a voice of their own for the Library, Shared and the news: whatever you do not change follows the general voice, and each book or shared item can have its own with Its voice. Remove everything of its own goes back to the inherited voice.
  • Optional ElevenLabs voices with your own API key (Settings, General): very natural voices, with speed and pitch, and what has been read is kept so it does not use credits again. If it fails (no credits or no connection), it says so and carries on with the phone's voice.
  • Braille books: ONCE BRA and BAN files, BRF and PEF are read as text (uncontracted Spanish braille), with their pages and headings.
  • EPUB 3 with synchronised narration (text and human voice): they open in the player and the text follows the voice, like a DAISY book.
  • Read with Lector Universal: select text in any app and choose it from the selection menu.
  • Continuous reading: what is pending in Shared, or a whole folder, read aloud one after another.
  • Pause reading or audio when the screen turns on or the phone is unlocked (optional).
  • Mono audio for audiobooks, podcasts and radio, for people who use a single earphone or hear less on one side.
  • More legible fonts (Atkinson Hyperlegible, OpenDyslexic, Lexend or your own) and custom colours that tell you their contrast with the background.
  • News and Podcasts each refresh at their own pace: from every 5 minutes to once a day, or every so many minutes of your choice.
  • Go to a time with jumps from where you are (+5:00, -1:30) or to a part of the total (50 %).
  • A more complete Shared: articles show author, outlet, date and listening minutes; a warning if you already have it; Download again; offline, the link is saved and downloaded later on its own; favourites; sorting; offers to save a copied link; and shortcuts from the app icon.
  • Podcasts: per-podcast volume and search in fyyd and gpodder too. News: groups.io groups, Mark the ones above as read, and rules match whole words (with * at the end, also inside other words, like educa*).
  • Radio recordings: skipping forward and back no longer jumps to the start, and their length is known.
  • Settings: one tab per section, Shared included, in the order of your sections; from a section's side menu they open on that section's tab. The bottom tabs show their full name, on two lines if needed.
  • The notifications permission is only asked for when needed, explaining why.
  • If the app closes because of an error, the report is kept only on your phone and you decide whether to send it (Settings, General, Report a problem). The app never sends anything on its own.
  • Full review of every section with many fixes: playback, timers, snoozed alarm, voice, TalkBack focus, catalogues (passwords only go to their own server), watched folders, translated texts and more. Slow speech engines no longer freeze the app.

Version 1.0.11

  • Library by type: Books, Documents, Audiobooks and Web pages, each on its own tab at the top of the Library, plus All. You can create your own types (Notes, Magazines, Scores…). Tabs are always fully visible: the ones that do not fit go into the last one, More.
  • Each type has its own folders: a folder created in Documents only appears in Documents (and in All). What you add on a type's tab or in one of its folders gets that type, and the Change type action changes it, also for several items or a whole folder.
  • In Settings, Library, Library tabs and types: which tabs are shown, in which order, and create, rename or delete your own types.
  • The Library list no longer jumps with many books or folders: when it refreshes, TalkBack focus stays where you were, and the count is no longer repeated on every change. It also loads much faster.
  • Search for books on the whole device (optional, in Add books): all your EPUB and PDF files, or all books and documents, whatever folder they are in, to open them or add them to the library at once. Only then is access to files requested.
  • New Shared section: what you share with the app from other apps or from the browser (an article, a text, a file) arrives here, with the To read, Read and All tabs, without mixing with the library. Move to the library lets you choose its type and folder if you want to keep it. Read items can be deleted automatically.
  • In Android's Share menu, the app appears as Lector Universal: read, Lector Universal: subscribe in News, Lector Universal: subscribe in Podcasts and Lector Universal: add to Radio, with their icons, so they are not confused with other apps.
  • A voice for each book: engine, voice, speed, pitch, voice change by language and pause between paragraphs. Whatever you do not change follows the general voice in Settings. From the Its voice button while reading or with the Voice for this book action.
  • News voice: engine, voice, speed, pitch and pauses for the news (Settings, News). At the end of an article it says End of the article, not End of the book.
  • New Settings, with tabs at the bottom: General, Library, Podcasts, News and Radio, each with its sections.
  • Radio: many more stations now play. The app identifies itself with its own name (some servers rejected Android's), follows redirects between http and https, tries without encryption if the station's certificate is not valid, and includes a backup decoder for formats such as the MP2 of some HLS stations. If a station cannot play, it says why.
  • Radio: when adding a station any address works (MP3, AAC, Ogg, Opus, FLAC, HLS, PLS, M3U, ASX and XSPF playlists) or the station's web page: the app finds the stream in it, checks it and takes the name the station gives.
  • News: you can follow a YouTube channel by sharing any of its videos, also from the YouTube app, and videos are read with their description. Better RSS discovery for a website, a clear message for networks without RSS, and read items that are cleaned up no longer come back as new.
  • Top podcasts and the stations of your country now come from the phone's SIM and network, not the language: phones set to Spanish (United States) got the United States podcasts, in English. In Discover you can choose the country.

Version 1.0.10

  • New backup manager (Settings, Backups): choose what to save (library, books saved in the app, podcasts, news, radio, recordings, statistics, settings, and keys and passwords) and, when restoring, what to recover. Use it to move to a new phone and have everything exactly the same.
  • Backups can be protected with a password: they are encrypted with 256-bit AES. Keys and passwords are only saved in password-protected backups.
  • Before anything is changed, the whole backup is checked, and before restoring, how everything was is saved so you can undo it.
  • AI keys and passwords are stored separately and are no longer included in the Android backup (your Google account) unless you turn it on. That backup now also includes podcasts, radio and statistics.
  • The telemetry that came with Google's text and language recognition libraries is turned off: nothing is sent any more. Thanks to Jesús Pavón Abián.
  • Reading aloud can no longer close the app if language detection fails. Thanks to Jesús Pavón Abián.

Version 1.0.9

  • New Home screen: the app opens on a screen with the most useful things from each section (the books you are reading, the episodes in progress, queued or new, the latest unread news and your favourite stations). Each section has its heading, to jump with TalkBack, and double-tapping it opens the whole section.
  • Back from the main screen of any section goes to Home, and from Home it leaves the app.
  • Settings, Home and sections: choose the order of the sections (side menu, Home and statistics), which ones appear on Home and how many items from each. Sections are moved with TalkBack actions.
  • Statistics for every section: besides reading, the time listening to podcasts and the radio and the time with the news, with streaks, episodes finished, articles opened, most played stations and more.
  • Updates from your server, from GitHub, from both (the newest version) or from a custom address (Settings, General). The app is now on GitHub as a public project.
  • The reading screen no longer has a Settings button: Settings are only in the side menu.

Version 1.0.8

  • New Radio section (side menu): more than 50,000 stations from around the world from the free radio-browser.info directory, with no account and no ads. Tabs Favourites (with folders), Explore (your country, most listened to, most voted, trending, what people are listening to now, and by genre, country or language), Search with filters, History (stations, songs and recordings) and Schedule.
  • Radio: live player that reconnects by itself and announces the song that is playing, previous and next station (also with headphones), sleep timer, equalizer and searching the song online.
  • Radio: record a station with no loss of quality, even while you listen to something else, and scheduled recordings.
  • Radio: alarm clock with your station, on the days you choose, with the volume rising gradually and Turn off and Snooze even when the phone is locked. Without internet, the phone’s alarm sound plays.
  • Radio: stations by hand (also from .pls or .m3u), M3U import and export, “Add to Radio” from Share and home screen shortcuts.
  • Mini player above the tabs in every section: what is playing (a book or article read aloud, an audiobook, an episode or the radio), Pause or Resume and Stop; tapping it goes back to its screen. Only one thing plays at a time.
  • The notification and the lock screen always open what is playing. Podcasts and radio in HLS (.m3u8) can now be played.
  • Each field has its own keyboard: numbers for percentages and paragraphs, time for times, and the address keyboard only for addresses.
  • Fixes: the widget no longer closes the app with an audiobook; Podcasts no longer closes when leaving Discover while loading; episodes and articles no longer offer bookmarks that were not saved; the “end of episode” timer is announced correctly; going back to an older version no longer closes the app. Opening an article in the browser marks it as read.

Version 1.0.7

  • Your own library websites: in Catalogs, “Add catalog or library website” with the address of any online library. The app finds out whether it is an OPDS catalog or a website; websites open in the app browser, like Tiflolibros, and whatever you download (books, documents or ZIP files) goes to your library.
  • Fixed: after editing Tiflolibros or the ONCE Digital Library, they no longer opened as a website.
  • About shows the author’s name, Héctor J. Benítez Corredera, and the current sections and formats.

Version 1.0.6

  • New Podcasts section (side menu): Queue, Inbox, Subscriptions, Downloads, Favorites, History and Discover. Search, top podcasts by category, OPML, a queue that continues on its own, downloads, speed and skips per podcast, chapters, notes and transcripts.
  • New News section (side menu): Unread, Feeds by category, Favorites, Read later and Search. Articles no longer go to the library unless you use “Save to the library”; the reader has Next, Previous, Favorite, Save and Web buttons.
  • Search feeds by topic, recommended Spanish feeds, full articles, read all unread aloud, hide by words and notifications per feed.
  • News and podcasts refresh on their own every so often.
  • Sync with your account: news with FreshRSS, The Old Reader, Miniflux or Nextcloud News; podcasts with gpodder.net or Nextcloud (subscriptions and where you are in each episode). In Settings, News and Podcasts, “Account”.
  • Chapters embedded in the episode audio (MP3 and M4A), even without downloading it. Transcripts say who is speaking.
  • Library, Podcasts and News work the same way: “Open menu” at the top and their tabs at the bottom, all visible. Online catalogs are now a tab of the Library.
  • Podcasts: full page before subscribing, with all episodes playable without subscribing; search episodes; see chapters without playing; play continuously from an episode; keep only the latest downloads.
  • News: show today’s or read articles, saved searches, keyword rules and alerts, trash, feed status, AI summary and export.
  • Lists keep TalkBack’s place when they refresh. Settings only in the side menu.
  • Player redesigned and consistent: buttons that say exactly what they do, accessible position bar with the time, speed in exact steps of 5 and full information. Settings sliders say their value with units.
  • Improved full article (several pages, no “related news”, download when refreshing, subscribers-only notice) and sign in to the news sites you subscribe to.
  • Throughout the app, when a dialog or menu closes, TalkBack focus returns to the item you opened it from. Slow actions (subscribing, searching, refreshing, checking for updates, downloading) show a wait dialog with Cancel and the result appears right there. Actions change with the state: “See in Subscriptions” if you are already subscribed, “Remove from favorites”, “Already in your library: open it”.
  • Customisable TalkBack actions everywhere in the app (Settings, TalkBack actions): choose which appear and in what order for books, folders, paragraphs, bookmarks, catalogs, articles, feeds, categories, podcasts and episodes.
  • Side menu like Material 3’s: when it opens, focus goes to the section you are in, which says “selected”; the others say nothing and open with a single double-tap.
  • When returning from a dialog (for example, describing an image with AI) the book title is no longer repeated and TalkBack keeps moving through the actions without spelling. Read aloud recovers by itself if the voice engine does not deliver the first sentence.
  • Folders in News and Podcasts: create, rename, reorder and delete them (asking whether to delete their contents too or leave them without a folder). “Move to a folder” on every feed and podcast, with the option to create a new one by typing its name; when you subscribe you are asked which folder.
  • Import OPML with a review first: it detects what is news and what is a podcast (also in mixed files) and you choose which to import, where each goes, its folder and its name.
  • Podcasts: filter Inbox, Queue and Episodes by folder, “In progress” view, time left in the queue and show-note timestamps that jump to that moment.
  • News: subscribe from other apps’ Share menu, YouTube, Reddit, Mastodon and Bluesky feeds, start reading an article as soon as it opens (setting) and confirmation before marking a whole folder or feed as read.
  • Read aloud can skip web addresses, parentheses and square brackets. New catalog: Internet Archive (free books, also Spanish only).
  • Voice-guided scanner that works without Google services: it tells you how to move the phone and takes the photo only when the whole page is in view.
  • Library: “Want to read” and “Abandoned” statuses; Authors, Series and Collections views (a book can be in several collections); the series is filled in automatically if the book includes it.
  • Reader: highlights of a paragraph or a sentence, with categories, that you can view, edit and export; math formulas are read in words.
  • News: “Read headlines” and Undo after marking in bulk; “Read all” marks as read only the articles you listened to. Podcasts: transcripts with each paragraph’s time as a link to that moment.
  • Android Auto has been removed.
  • Tiflolibros and the ONCE Digital Library inside the app (in Catalogs): you sign in with your account and whatever you download is added to your library, including ZIPs with audiobooks or DAISY.
  • Podcasts: sleep timer at the end of the episode or after several episodes, and an automatic night-time timer; playback pauses when headphones are unplugged and can resume when they reconnect.
  • Podcasts: filters by words and minimum length, episodes from oldest to newest, sorting subscriptions and the queue, private podcasts with user name and password, and failed downloads retry by themselves.
  • News: a notification for each article with “Mark as read” and “Add to favorites”; change a feed’s address; the voice reads each feed in its language; reading time; keep only articles with some words and skip what came from another feed.
  • Reader: navigate by formulas, code and separators; “Leave the table, list or quote”; copy several paragraphs; quick mark.
  • Formats: Word 97-2003 (DOC), comics (CBZ) with description and text recognition for each page, and old MS-DOS TXT files with their accents. Opening a web address that is a document saves it to the library with its name.

Version 1.0.4

  • When TalkBack speaks, the book lowers its volume instead of going quiet, and goes back to normal when TalkBack finishes. It works even if TalkBack's own audio ducking is off. You can change it in Settings, “When TalkBack speaks”.
  • Voice and speed for this book works: it applies right away, even while reading, tells you the chosen voice and speed and has “Try the voice of this book”.
  • Where am I and other messages are no longer repeated twice with TalkBack.
  • Reading buttons without duplicates: every action is in the buttons at the top, in two rows, and only what doesn't fit goes to the More menu. In Settings, Navigation and TalkBack, “Button rows while reading”.
  • Describe images: on an image, the “Describe the image” action sends it to another app (Be My Eyes, Gemini, Lookout, Seeing AI...) or opens it to view it. Images without a description in EPUB books now appear.

Version 1.0.3

  • Play and Pause for read aloud are always consistent: Pause keeps the book paused even if TalkBack speaks afterwards; Play keeps it playing.
  • When TalkBack speaks, the book goes quiet and resumes on its own when TalkBack finishes, if it was playing.
  • When pausing, TalkBack no longer reads the whole paragraph. To go there, use the “Go to the paragraph being read” action on the Voice or Pause button.
  • With TalkBack on, the sentence is no longer highlighted on screen, so TalkBack does not read it again.
  • The notification shows the same state as the app.

Version 1.0.2

  • New, more stable read aloud: the app generates the audio of the next sentences in advance and plays it itself. TalkBack no longer freezes after unlocking the screen, the Play/Pause button always shows the real state, and pausing stops mid-sentence.
  • When TalkBack speaks, the book goes quiet and resumes on its own when TalkBack finishes. In Settings, “When TalkBack speaks” lets you choose: pause and resume, lower the volume, or keep playing as is.
  • New option in Settings, General: “Keep battery saving from stopping reading”.
  • Configurable pause between sentences.
  • Previous sentence goes back to the start of the sentence if it has been playing for a while; next sentence jumps instantly.

  • Remove books from the Reading list or clear it.

Version 1.0.0

First version.

  • Library with Reading, Library, Folders and Bookmarks tabs, and a side menu.
  • Nested folders to organize your books; when you add a folder from the device, its subfolders can become library folders. Watched folders that add new books automatically.
  • Reading designed for TalkBack: one paragraph per item, real headings with their level, links, footnotes in a window and position saved where the focus is.
  • Navigation bar with an adjustable selector (swipe up or down) for headings, levels, chapters, pages, links, lists, tables, quotes, images, notes, bookmarks, results and percentage. Optional volume keys and physical keyboard shortcuts.
  • Table of contents, go to a percentage, page or paragraph, list of elements, where am I and jump history.
  • Search with history, match case, whole words, regular expressions and accent-insensitive matching.
  • Bookmarks and notes with sound, exportable.
  • Read aloud sentence by sentence with the installed voices: speed, pitch, pauses, highlighting, voice switching by language, pronunciation dictionary, voice and speed per book, sleep timer, notification and headphone buttons.
  • Audiobook and DAISY player with chapters, levels, pages, sentences, speed without pitch change, skips, bookmarks, sleep timer, skip silence and volume boost.
  • Formats: EPUB, PDF, Word, OpenDocument, RTF, text, HTML, MHT, Markdown, FictionBook, Kindle (MOBI, AZW3), CHM, PowerPoint, Excel, ODP, ODS, CSV, DAISY 2.02 and 3, M4B, MP3 and more.
  • Reading statistics, backup, app in Spanish and English (translatable) and updates from the server.
